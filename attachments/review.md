# Review for Hugh: check that a merge can finish before landing it

Recommendation: make the existing merge preflight check the authority needed by its required succession acts before it reports “allowed” or advances the source branch. When it cannot finish under the merging actor, show the exact predecessor, path and actor or role needed. Keep this in the existing merge plan; add no task state or approval form.

The Notes delivery shows the cost of discovering this late. Its saved plan for cc24d6c4 reports allowed=true, including succession_allowed and reviewed_scope_allowed. It includes four Claude-authored predecessors across two deleted bundle paths, with no successor at those paths. The source then landed at b2ed9266 and all 24 successors were published, but gs merge could not finish because those four retirements were ineffective. Codex had to request separate author/ratifier cleanup. Source delivery, record completion and the next live-reader update consequently stopped at different points. These are observed results; I have not measured a time saving or established how often this occurs.

The source explains the mismatch. [ValidateSuccession and ValidateReach](https://github.com/generalbusiness-ai/gitseq/blob/b2ed92662464b9ca7670395fce26fe723fa069d1/internal/mergeplan/mergeplan.go#L782) check citations, successor publication and reviewed path reach. [preflightBatchAdmission](https://github.com/generalbusiness-ai/gitseq/blob/b2ed92662464b9ca7670395fce26fe723fa069d1/cmd/gs/main.go#L1918) constructs acts and checks their size, but does not establish each act’s projected effectiveness. The [fold’s authority rule](https://github.com/generalbusiness-ai/gitseq/blob/b2ed92662464b9ca7670395fce26fe723fa069d1/internal/workroom/fold.go#L1454) deliberately reserves a retirement without a successor to the artifact’s author or a ratifier. A path lying inside the reviewed scope is not sufficient authority for that act.

The small first correction is an accurate preflight: this exact fixture should identify the four required retirements before any source ref or approval reservation becomes permanent. A positive control should show an authorized plan still completes. Keep the existing signature, exact-path, review, custody and retirement boundaries, and retain the resumable path for genuinely interrupted deliveries. Do not turn this into a general grant to retire another actor’s artifacts. If the existing authority model makes ordinary reviewed deletions needlessly dependent on another actor, that deserves a separate explicit policy decision, not an accidental exception in the CLI.

Keep required destination succession separate from cleanup of historical abandoned candidates in the plan’s explanation. The same Notes follow-up also lists six Claude-authored abandoned candidates; those are additional cleanup obligations, not the four records that prevented this merge from completing. Carried and protected sibling candidates must retain their existing treatment.

This recommendation is distinct from the push race that later forked the sequence. The earlier incomplete merge led into cleanup work, but the reproduced cause of the fork was a successful Git push overwriting a concurrently advanced tracking destination. Fixing this preflight does not repair that publication defect.
