assertion in its named test. A compilation failure is not a kill: the named
test never runs, so it proves nothing about that test. Rounds 1 to 3 recorded

both outcomes. With that repair, all 29 entries in this file (28 from rounds 1
to 3, plus the round-4 empty-selection guard) are behavioural kills: every
mutant compiles and its named test fails on an assertion. Two round-1 entries
and five round-3 entries were withdrawn rather than repaired, for the reasons
stated at their rounds; withdrawn entries are not counted.
