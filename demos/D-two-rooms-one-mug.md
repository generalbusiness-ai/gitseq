# D. Two rooms, one mug

**Audience:** platform people; us. The liveness spectacle.
**Unique property staged:** the durability/liveness split, and entity-own-log custody.

## Scene (2 minutes)

1. Two browser windows, two rooms, live presence and chat.
2. A mug — an entity with its own log — is handed between rooms:
   offer, accept, settle; the losing concurrent claim stays visible
   as an attempt.
3. **The wow:** kill the server mid-conversation. Presence and chat
   vanish like a dropped call; every durable act survives; a fresh
   clone replays the custody chain offline.

## Needs beyond the current build

Multi-room (multiple logs served together) and the custody saga as a
UI flow — the spike proved the fold; the workroom serves one log.

## Disposition

**Fold into B and C rather than staging alone.** The server-kill beat
is demo B's step 5; visible losing attempts are demo C's core. Keep
this note as the reminder that the mug (entity-own-log custody)
deserves a walk-on role once multi-log serving exists.

**Status:** deliberately unstaged; parts absorbed elsewhere.
