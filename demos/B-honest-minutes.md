# B. Minutes that are honest about memory

**Audience:** teams drowning in meetings — the attestation ladder as UX.
**Unique property staged:** honest forgetting; promotion with verifiable evidence.

## Scene (3 minutes)

1. A short discussion happens in the room's ephemeral chat (live or
   scripted); an agent states extracted decision candidates as they
   emerge.
2. A human selects the frames that matter and sets down the decision —
   the complete signed frames ride along as evidence.
3. Everyone leaves. **The room forgets the conversation** — by
   contract, visibly.
4. **The wow:** later, someone disputes "that's not what we agreed."
   The decision event replays its embedded signed frames — verifiable
   by a stranger against the nexus key — while the surrounding chatter
   is honestly gone. Memory where it was promised, amnesia where it
   wasn't.
5. Kill the nexus mid-demo (demo D's best beat): presence and chat
   vanish; the durable record stands; restart resumes cold.

## Needs beyond the current build

A frame-evidence viewer (render + verify `frames.json` from an
event's attachments) — the embedding and signatures already exist.

**Status:** nearly free. Promotion-with-evidence and amnesia are
built and were demonstrated by accident during UI testing.
